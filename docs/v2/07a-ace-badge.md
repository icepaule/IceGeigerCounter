# ACE two-color rear badge v1.5.2

The Kobra S1 + ACE Pro variant of the rear badge is stored under `hardware/v2/field_case_v151/badge_v152/`.

Use the two generated meshes as parts of one object:

- `IceGeiger_Badge_v1.5.2_BASE.stl` — black background
- `IceGeiger_Badge_v1.5.2_LOGO_TEXT.stl` — yellow radiation symbol and `IceDrone`

The second-color geometry keys 0.40 mm into the base and extends 0.90 mm above it. Both files share the same origin and coordinates. Import them together in Anycubic Slicer Next, keep the original coordinates, then assign the two meshes to separate ACE slots.

The Field Case v1.5.1 enclosure geometry does not change; only the rear badge is replaced.
